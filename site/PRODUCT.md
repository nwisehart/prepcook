# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

Scope: the Prepcook website at prepcook.dev (`site/`). The board app
(`packages/local-web`, `packages/ui`, `packages/web-core`) has no product
record yet and does not inherit this one.

## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary: the team lead who decides whether a team adopts Prepcook.** They
own or oversee a bug queue on a product team that tracks work in Jira or
GitHub Issues, already uses coding agents, and has learned not to trust an
agent-written diff on its own. They come to the site to judge whether the
method is sound, what it costs to stand up, and how much control the team
keeps. They are the one who configures integrations and chooses between Chef
mode and Prep mode.

**Secondary: the engineers who will work the board.** They own tickets,
approve root-cause analyses, review fixes and take cards over. On the site
they read the docs: the quickstart, the method, configuration and the API.

## Product Purpose

Prepcook turns a bug report into a reviewed pull request while the engineer
who owns the ticket keeps the final say. The bottleneck in a bug queue is not
the typing but the reproduction nobody sets up, the root-cause analysis nobody
writes down and the review that queues behind everything else. Prepcook puts
those steps in a fixed order, each worked by a separate coding agent in its
own git worktree, on a local board where every card's state is visible and a
person can take any card over.

The site exists to let a lead understand that method and decide to try it,
and to carry the full documentation for the team that then runs it. Success
is a lead who reaches the quickstart convinced enough to run it on the Pantry
demo.

## Positioning

Reproduce first, then explain, then fix, then review with fresh eyes. The
planning agent must produce a harness that fails for the reported reason
before it looks for a cause; a person approves the root-cause analysis before
any fix is made; the reviewer works in a fresh worktree with none of the
planning context. Every approved analysis goes into a knowledge graph of root
causes, so a cause that keeps coming back opens a pattern review. Prepcook
works alongside people rather than around them: an engineer who assigns a
ticket to themselves takes it over and Prepcook steps aside.

## Operating Context

- Prepcook runs locally: `npx prepcook up` (or the release binaries) starts a
  board in Docker and a native server, then opens the UI. macOS or Linux,
  Docker, Node 20+, git, a signed-in coding agent CLI, and `gh` for pull
  requests.
- A card moves through Plan, Execute, Review, Shipped and Clean up. Plan
  stands the app up in the card's worktree from the repository's
  `.prepcook/scripts.toml`, reproduces the bug and writes the RCA.
- Integrations: Jira, GitHub Issues and PagerDuty for tickets; Slack, signed
  webhook, Discord and Microsoft Teams for notifications; Sentry, DataSet,
  Datadog, CloudWatch Logs and log files for evidence; nine coding agents,
  chosen per stage.
- Investigation cards, filed through the card API or from an incident, run
  Plan and stop at a triage report until a person converts them to a bug.
- The local toolkit runs the same loop at the terminal as Claude Code skills,
  without the board.
- The quickstart takes about 20 minutes of the user's own time plus 30 to 60
  minutes of agent time, on the Pantry demo repository.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is static, generated by `site/build.mjs` into `site/dist` from the
  repository's own docs (`README.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `docs/*.md`) plus
  hand-written pages in `site/src` (`home.html`, `tammy.html`,
  `vibe-kanban.html`). The home page's pitch, the "why the name" lines and
  the board caption are pulled from the README at build time; change the
  README, not the template, to change them. Every `docs/*.md` must have a
  sidebar entry or the build fails.
- Every link and asset reference is relative so `dist` works from any host;
  only canonical and Open Graph URLs, JSON-LD, the sitemap and robots use the
  absolute `https://prepcook.dev/`. The link check fails the build on any
  broken reference.
- Screenshots come in light and dark pairs (`docs/images/*.png` and
  `*-dark.png`) and are regenerated per `docs/images/README.md`.
- Tammy is a separate product in its own repository (`nwisehart/tammy`): a
  Slack assistant that answers customers from the docs and files
  investigation cards on Prepcook. The site gives her one page.
- Prepcook is open source under Apache 2.0 and is a fork of vibe-kanban by
  BloopAI; the site's vibe-kanban page states what was kept and what was
  added, and must keep doing so.
- Terminology: **Chef mode** (autonomy off) and **Prep mode** (autonomy on);
  **RCA**; **Needs you** for the attention state; **pull request** on
  GitHub and **merge request** on GitLab.

## Brand Commitments

- The name is **Prepcook**, one word, only the P capitalised, in every string
  and locale. "prep cook" (two words, lowercase) is the kitchen job, never the
  product. In code and on disk it is `prepcook`.
- The kitchen metaphor is the name's origin and is used sparingly: a prep cook
  does the prep so the chef can cook. Tammy extends it as the waitress.
- Voice: plain, declarative, specific. Claims are stated as mechanisms, not
  adjectives. Sentence case, no emoji, no exclamation marks.
- Brand assets in `assets/brand/`: the logo and mark (light, dark and mono)
  and the app icon. Tammy's logo and mark live in `site/assets/`.

## Evidence on Hand

- Prepcook is in use on a real team's bug queue. That may be said, but the
  team is not named and no numbers are quoted.
- The Pantry demo (`nwisehart/prepcook-demo`) and its `BUG.md` walk a real
  bug from report to merged pull request.
- Light and dark screenshots of the board, an RCA on a card, a triaged
  investigation, the stand-up check and the integration settings in
  `docs/images/`.
- The full documentation in `docs/`.
- Absent, and never to be invented: customer names or logos, testimonials,
  adoption or usage numbers, time-saved or accuracy benchmarks, pricing,
  and hosted or enterprise offerings.

## Product Principles

1. **The engineer keeps the final say.** Every claim and screen should show
   where a person approves, takes over or sends work back.
2. **Show the mechanism, not the promise.** Explain how a card moves and what
   each stage must prove; never claim outcomes there is no evidence for.
3. **Reproduce before explaining.** The order of the method is the product,
   and the site presents it in that order.
4. **The docs are the product's truth.** The site is built from the
   repository's own documentation, so the two never drift apart.
5. **Honest lineage.** Prepcook is open source and derived from vibe-kanban,
   and says so plainly.
